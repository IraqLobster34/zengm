## Title

I added an in-season NBA Cup to Basketball GM

## Post

I've been working on an unofficial NBA Cup mod for Basketball GM.

It has a dedicated League → NBA Cup page with six groups, conference wild cards, and a playoff-style knockout bracket. Every team still gets 82 counting games; the Cup final is an extra game that does not count toward regular-season records or stats.

The winning roster gets NBA Cup Winner awards on player profiles, and there's an NBA Cup MVP based on performance across the tournament. Cup wins appear in team history, league history, and season summaries too.

Screenshots and setup: https://github.com/IraqLobster34/zengm/tree/mods

You'll need Node.js 24 to run a private local copy. Start a 30-team basketball league with two 15-team conferences and an 82-game season, then open League → NBA Cup after preseason. The draw uses the NBA's five seeded pots based on last season's conference standings, with one team from each pot per group. A first season without history uses a random draw. The calendar and group tiebreaks remain adapted for ZenGM.
